changelog=2.0
    - New interface built for projects with dozens of themes: filterable theme list, layer tree drawn like the Layers panel, several themes compared side by side
    - Groups can be checked/unchecked per theme (Ctrl+click switches a whole branch), layer style per theme
    - Themes are repaired automatically when a group is renamed or moved; removed layers are remembered and can be restored in their themes
    - Export/import of several themes at once, one mapping dialog instead of one popup per layer, passwords removed from exported data sources
    - Non-modal window, Russian and French translations
